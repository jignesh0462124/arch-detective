# Reference thesis format inspection

## Scope

Reference: `C:/Users/jigne/Downloads/G-24 TrueSheild - AI.pdf`.

Inspected on 15 November 2026. The PDF contains 93 pages and was exported from Microsoft Word 2021. All pages were text-extracted and visually surveyed in contact sheets. Representative cover, body, chapter-divider, table, figure, index and reference pages were also rendered at larger size and inspected. This was a format study, not verification of the reference project's claims or a line-by-line content proofread. The reference PDF was not changed.

## Observed format

- Portrait A4: 595.32 × 841.92 PDF points, approximately 210 × 297 mm.
- Black multi-line rectangular page border on most main report pages; not uniform on the final appendix placeholders.
- Asymmetric page layout with a wide left binding margin. Many body lines begin around x = 108–116 pt and extend no further than about x = 533 pt. The frame is approximately x = 101–547 pt, y = 61–766 pt on representative pages. These are measured PDF coordinates, **not recovered Microsoft Word margin settings**.
- Predominantly Calibri body text at 12 pt. Some paragraphs, tables, references and captions use 10 pt; this varies across sections.
- Numbered subsection headings are generally Calibri Bold 14 pt, with some 12 pt exceptions.
- Chapter-divider titles are approximately Calibri Bold 16 pt, uppercase and horizontally centred around the middle of a separate page.
- Cover project title is Calibri Bold 18 pt. Cover content is centred between institutional branding at top/bottom, with project name, degree, programme, authors, guide and submission month/year.
- Body text is predominantly left-aligned with a ragged right edge, rather than uniformly justified.
- Many 12 pt paragraphs have approximately 22 pt line-to-line spacing, consistent with roughly 1.5-line Word spacing; some sections use tighter spacing. Exact paragraph settings cannot be recovered reliably from a PDF alone.
- Main-body page numbers are centred near the bottom, inside the page frame. Preliminary numbering is inconsistent with the index.
- Figures and screenshots are centred. Most captions appear below figures, in a chapter-based pattern such as Figure 3.1. Some figures instead contain their own embedded heading/caption.
- Tables use black grid lines, bold header text, chapter-based numbering and usually a caption above the table. Some tables continue across pages.
- References use bracketed numeric identifiers such as [1], [2], with visible hyperlinks and mixed italic/regular formatting. This resembles an IEEE-style numeric bibliography but was not checked against the formal style specification.

## What to reproduce in ThreatLens

- Overall A4 layout, institutional front-page arrangement, page frame, Calibri typography and separate chapter-divider pages.
- Numbered headings, readable paragraphs, centred figures and consistent chapter-based captions.
- Grid-style tables, numeric references and clearly identified appendices.
- Rebuild the contents, List of Figures and List of Tables from the completed ThreatLens document.
- Preserve the user's ThreatLens/Archon terminology, actual authors, guide, department, results and award information. Do not copy names, signatures, results, charts or prose from the reference thesis.

## Inconsistencies not to reproduce

- The index lists chapter ranges that do not match the exported PDF's actual chapter locations.
- Preliminary entries use Roman-number references in the index while visible pages use continuous Arabic numbers.
- Inconsistent subsection sizes, paragraph spacing, caption placement and figure/table numbering.
- Repeated headings and table captions; blank table rows and uneven cell wrapping.
- Some diagrams are too dense or have small text at final page size.
- The last appendix pages are placeholders, not completed evidence.
